Quick but important update. LearnQt is being rebranded as [Squared Academy](https://squared.academy/), the training arm of our company, [Squared Computing](https://squared.co.ke/).

<img src="/assets/img/blog/squared-academy-rebrand/cover.png" alt="LearnQt becomes Squared Academy: same Qt, C++ and QML, now down to the hardware" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; width: 700px;">

## Why

Here's the honest story. For the last 3 or 4 years, my work has taken me down the rabbit hole of embedded hardware and firmware. Boards, sensors, microcontrollers, the kind of devices that don't have a desktop to draw a window on.

Qt, C++ and QML are still what I'm known for, and they're still a big part of what I do. But the scope of what we have to offer has outgrown the LearnQt name. When most of what I'm teaching and building now touches hardware, a brand that says "Qt" and nothing else doesn't tell the whole story anymore. So the name changes to match the work.

## What this means for you

**1. The Qt, C++ and QML content doesn't change.** The content we're usually known for stays exactly as it is. It only reaches deeper now, and gets to cover all those weird hardware devices where Qt and C++ end up running.

**2. Courses and books are moving to Squared Academy.** All of our courses and books will gradually move to [squared.academy](https://squared.academy/). We're building this platform to be not noisy, and to give you all the info you need in one place. Until now, the videos lived on [Teachable](https://www.teachable.com/) and the books on [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/). All of that will get a single digital home.

**3. Our channels become Squared Academy.** YouTube, LinkedIn, Discord, X and the others will all carry the Squared Academy name. I will try my best to share as much info as I can about the change as it happens. If you face any issue along the way, just shoot me an email at [daniel.g@learnqt.guide](mailto:daniel.g@learnqt.guide) and I will do the best I can to help out.

## A change to the emails you get from us

One more thing worth knowing. All future emails from us will come from a squared.academy address, not from learnqt.guide as they have for the last couple of years. If you're on our mailing list, you'll start seeing the new sender soon. It's the same me writing, just under a new domain.

So if you stop seeing our emails, check your spam folder and mark the new address as safe. That way a course update or a new book announcement doesn't get lost.

## And no, we're not leaving Udemy

I know some of you are wondering, so let me say it plainly: we are not stopping our work on Udemy. I'm in fact updating the C++ 23 Masterclass to be 2026 worthy as we speak. If you're enrolled in that course, be sure to [check it out](https://www.udemy.com/course/the-modern-cpp-20-masterclass).

## What to do now

Nothing, really. Your courses and books are still where you left them, and the move will be gradual. I'll announce each step as it happens, so you're never left hunting for something.

If you want to see where this is headed, have a look at [squared.academy](https://squared.academy/).

Thank you for learning with me all these years. The best parts are still ahead.

Happy coding!
